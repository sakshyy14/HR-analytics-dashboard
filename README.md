# 👥 HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive **HR Analytics project built in Microsoft Power BI** to analyze employee demographics, satisfaction, performance, workforce distribution, and attrition patterns. The project transforms a **1,470-record HR dataset sourced from Kaggle** into a multi-page, decision-ready dashboard that helps HR teams understand workforce trends and identify factors associated with employee attrition.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Employee retention is an important challenge for organizations. Understanding **who is leaving, which departments and job roles experience higher attrition, and what employee characteristics are associated with turnover** can help HR teams make better workforce decisions.

This project simulates a real-world HR analytics scenario by taking raw employee data, preparing it using **Power Query**, creating analytical columns and measures using **DAX**, and presenting the results through an interactive **three-page Power BI dashboard**.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of employee demographics, job satisfaction, performance, salary, tenure, education, overtime, business travel, and attrition.

---

## 🎯 Business Questions Answered

* How many employees are currently in the organization?
* What is the overall employee attrition and attrition rate?
* How is the workforce distributed by gender, age, education, and marital status?
* Which job roles and departments have higher attrition?
* How does attrition vary across salary slabs?
* Which age groups experience the highest attrition?
* How does employee tenure relate to attrition?
* What are the job satisfaction and employee performance levels?
* How do overtime, business travel, and distance from office vary across employees?
* Which job roles have the highest average monthly income?

## 📊 Dashboard Structure

The dashboard consists of **3 interactive pages** connected through **page navigation buttons**.

### 🏠 1. HR Overview

The Home page provides an executive-level overview of the workforce.

**Key KPIs:**

* **1,470 Total Employees**
* **1,233 Active Employees**
* **882 Male Employees (60%)**
* **588 Female Employees (40%)**

**Key analysis:**

* Average Monthly Income by Job Role
* Job Satisfaction Rating by Job Role
* Employee Satisfaction
* Employee Performance
* Employees by Department

This page provides a quick snapshot of the organization's workforce and employee experience.

---

### 📉 2. Attrition Analysis

The Attrition page focuses on understanding **why employees leave the organization**.

**Key KPIs:**

* **237 Attritions**
* **16.1% Attrition Rate**
* **7.0 Average Years at Company**
* **37 Average Age**
* **6.5K Average Salary**
* **3 Average Training**

**Key analysis:**

* Attrition by Education
* Attrition by Age
* Attrition by Job Role
* Attrition by Salary Slab
* Attrition by Years at Company
* Attrition by Department

This page helps identify employee segments and organizational areas associated with higher attrition.

---

### 👥 3. Employee Analysis

The Detail page provides a deeper understanding of workforce demographics and employee characteristics.

**Key analysis:**

* Employee distribution by Age
* Marital Status
* Overtime participation
* Distance from Office
* Business Travel
* Education

This page helps HR teams understand workforce composition and explore employee characteristics that may influence engagement and retention.

## 📌 Key Dashboard Highlights

The dashboard provides insights such as:

* **16.1% overall attrition rate** across 1,470 employees.
* **237 employees** are represented in the attrition analysis.
* **Research & Development** has the largest workforce among the departments shown.
* Employees aged **26–35** form the largest age group.
* **Life Sciences** represents the largest education category.
* Most employees report **no overtime** and **very close proximity to the office**.
* Attrition is concentrated more heavily within certain **salary slabs, job roles, age groups, and departments**.
